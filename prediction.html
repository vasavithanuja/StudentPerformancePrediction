How to fill this form

Enter your actual academic information. CGPA and SGPA should be entered out of 10. Attendance should be entered as a percentage.

👤

Student Details

Basic academic information

Enter your full name. Enter your college roll number. Select your department. Select your current academic year. Select the semester you are currently studying.
📚

Academic Performance

Enter your actual academic records

Enter your current CGPA out of 10. Example: 8.32. Enter your SGPA from the previous semester, out of 10. Example: 8.10. Enter your average attendance percentage. Example: 83%. Enter the number of subjects you currently have as backlogs. If none, enter 0.
📖

Study Habits

Tell us about your regular routine

Enter approximately how many hours you study on a normal day. Example: 3 hours. Enter the number of separate study sessions you usually complete in one week. Example: 5. Enter your average sleep duration per day. Example: 7 hours.

This prototype uses your academic records and study habits to generate a performance estimate.
